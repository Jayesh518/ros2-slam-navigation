# Phase 3: baseline results

Configuration: **baseline** = shipped Nav2 1.3.13 defaults (NavFn planner, MPPI controller) with only the robot-specific edits (`configs/nav2/CHANGES.md`), SLAM Toolbox map plus AMCL, real-time factor 1. 20 start/goal pairs per world, 1 trial per pair, 60 trials. Trials ran 2026-10-26 in session order A, B, C (session seeds 11, 12, 13; trials shuffled within a session). Raw rows: `results/trials/baseline_world_*.csv`; tables generated by `scripts/analyse.py` into `results/summary.md`.

| world | trials | succeeded | success rate (Wilson 95% interval) | median time (successes) | median path | recoveries per trial | mean AMCL error |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 open room | 20 | 20 | 1.00 (0.84 to 1.00) | 44.4 s | 8.4 m | 0.10 | 0.13 m |
| B1 office | 20 | 16 | 0.80 (0.58 to 0.92) | 64.3 s | 13.0 m | 0.30 | 0.17 m |
| C1 pillar grid | 20 | 20 | 1.00 (0.84 to 1.00) | 57.8 s | 10.6 m | 1.35 | 0.13 m |
| all | 60 | 56 | 0.93 (0.84 to 0.97) | 53.9 s | 10.5 m | 0.58 | 0.14 m |

Collisions: 0 contact episodes in all 60 trials. No invalid trials; every reset was accepted at the first attempt.

## The four failures (all in world B)
| pair | outcome | path driven | AMCL error near the end | reading |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| b02 | timeout, 120 s | 18.1 m | up to 1.08 m (localisation failure by the protocol rule) | lost localisation, then wandered |
| b11 | timeout, 120 s | 18.4 m | up to 0.79 m (localisation failure) | same pattern |
| b04 | aborted by Nav2 at 90 s | 14.5 m | up to 0.43 m | planning/control (after recoveries) |
| b20 | timeout, 120 s | 18.0 m | up to 0.37 m | planning/control, no localisation failure |

So 2 of 4 failures coincide with large localisation error and 2 do not. With 4 failures this is a description, not a test.

## What this baseline can and cannot say
- Worlds A and C are at the ceiling (20 of 20). In those worlds a configuration cannot show an improvement in success rate, only a decrease or a change in time, path, recoveries. All the room to improve success is in world B (4 failures of 20).
- Preliminary, not a hypothesis test: H3 expected the highest localisation error and localisation-driven failures in the most symmetric world (C1). In the baseline, C1 has the same mean AMCL error as A1 (0.13 m) and no failures, while the highest error and all failures are in B1. H3 looks unsupported so far; the formal statement waits for the full study.
- Intervals are wide: 20 trials per world give a Wilson interval of about 0.84 to 1.00 even at 20 of 20.
- One trial per pair. Repeating a pair in the same session varied by about 2% in time (protocol change log (j)), measured on one pair only.
- Simulation only: LiDAR noise is Gaussian with 0.01 m standard deviation; there is no wheel slip model beyond the default friction, no lighting, no latency. Maps come from default SLAM Toolbox with a 3.5 m LiDAR; they are rotated 0.6 to 2.4 degrees from the world and have unobserved patches (`docs/figures/maps-slam.png`).
- Collisions: zero everywhere, so the collision metric carries no information for the baseline. It was verified to work in the test arena (wheel against a wall), not yet in a study world with a deliberate contact.
- AMCL, Nav2 and Gazebo physics are not bit-reproducible; re-running gives close but not identical numbers.
- The 0.5 m ground-truth arrival tolerance (protocol change (g)) was set before these trials; the exact final error of each trial is in the CSV.
